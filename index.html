Our Story & Space

Classic Patisserie Craft, Unapologetic Street Energy.

Born from a love affair between classic Parisian corner patisseries and modern street-graphic culture, Maison de Five is built around five pillars: love, quality, aroma, texture, and style.

Step under our Botticelli-striped awning into a sun-drenched room filled with the scent of caramelized French butter, vinyl soul tracks, and double-ristretto espresso shots pulled on custom brass levers. Whether you're grabbing a warm Shio Pan du classique on the run or lingering over a Burnt Vanilla Basque slice, this is your daily ritual elevated.
